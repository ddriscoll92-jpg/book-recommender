Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything UK primary teachers ask before getting started with LessonNest.

Is LessonNest aligned to the UK National Curriculum?

Yes. LessonNest is designed specifically for UK primary schools and generates content aligned to the KS1 and KS2 National Curriculum, including appropriate year group expectations, curriculum objectives, and vocabulary levels.

What year groups does LessonNest support?

LessonNest supports Year 1 through Year 6 (ages 5–11). Resources are tailored to the selected year group's reading level, vocabulary, and curriculum content.

Can I use any book, or only books in a list?

LessonNest has a large built-in library of books popular in UK primary schools, and you can also use the AI Assistant to work with any book or topic — even if it's not in the database.

Are the worksheets really differentiated?

Yes. Worksheets are generated with three levels — below expectation (support), at expectation (core), and above expectation (extension) — all from a single click, tailored to the lesson topic and year group.

Can I download resources as Word or PDF?

Yes. Most resources can be downloaded as PDF (ready to print) or Word (.docx) for further editing. Slideshows download as editable PowerPoint (.pptx) files.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — LessonNest offers a free trial with no credit card required. You can generate lesson plans and a set number of resources before choosing whether to upgrade to a Basic or Premium plan.

How is LessonNest different from ChatGPT or other AI tools?

LessonNest is purpose-built for UK primary teachers. Unlike generic AI tools, it understands the National Curriculum, generates properly formatted and printable resources (PDF, Word, PPTX), and organises everything into units of work — so you're not copying and pasting from a chat window into a worksheet template.
